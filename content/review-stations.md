# Review stations

**Today** you rotate through three stations, about 25 minutes each: find bad cables, fix broken circuits, and patch a studio on paper.

::: teacher
**DRAFT.** Owed: the studio's dead-cable bin (ask the studio staff), printed patchbay cards, and about 30 minutes before class to plant one fault in each kit.
:::

## Station 1: Cable Doctor
Use your breadboard continuity tester on real cables.

- Map an XLR cable pin by pin: 1 to 1, 2 to 2, 3 to 3.
- Find a short: the LED lights between two contacts that should never connect.
- Find an intermittent cable: hold the probes on and wiggle the cable. A flicker means a bad cable.

## Station 2: Troubleshoot boards
Each kit has one planted fault. Find it, fix it, and tell James what it was.

::: remember
When a circuit fails, remove parts until the problem is small enough to see.
:::

::: teacher
Fault ideas: an LED backwards, a part in the wrong strip, a missing jumper, a rail gap. One per kit.
:::

## Station 3: Paper patchbay
Lay out the cards as a working signal chain: mic → preamp → interface → monitors; guitar → DI; then the digital links.
